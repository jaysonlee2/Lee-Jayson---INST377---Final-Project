How to Use the App

First, look at the three starter Pokémon cards and compare their stats. Then use the sliders to decide which stats matter most to you. You only have 25 points, so you have to choose what is most important.

After that, answer the dropdown questions about your experience level, battle style, and starter goal. When you click “Get Recommendation,” the app combines your stat choices and playstyle answers to recommend Oshawott, Tepig, or Snivy.

Saving Choices

After getting a recommendation, click “Save Choice” to save it to the database. To view saved recommendations, click “Load Saved Choices.” The app will show the five newest saved choices.

Card Carousel

The trading card carousel shows Pokémon card images from TCGdex. You can use the arrows or swipe to browse cards for Oshawott, Tepig, and Snivy.
