Project Purpose

Unova Starter Finder helps players choose between Oshawott, Tepig, and Snivy from Pokémon Black and White. Instead of picking randomly, users can compare real Pokémon stats and answer playstyle questions.

The app uses PokéAPI for starter data, TCGdex for Pokémon card data, Chart.js for the stat chart, Swiper.js for the card carousel, and Supabase to save starter choices.

Information Problem

New or returning players may not know which starter fits their style. This app solves that problem by turning Pokémon data into a simple recommendation system based on stats and player preferences.

Pokémon data provided by PokéAPI and TCGdex | Saved with Supabase
